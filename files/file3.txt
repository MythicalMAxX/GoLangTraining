Promoted shelves are shelves that contain the many of the most popular books in Wikibooks.
See also featured books, for quality books that the community believes to be the best of what Wikibooks has to offer.

Shelf:Games has books on games, and includes the subsection shelves athletic games, board games, card games, electronic games, and game design.
Image credit

Shelf:Computer programming has books on computer programming, such as LaTeX, OpenSCAD User Manual, Python Programming, Java Programming, and C Programming.
Image credit

Shelf:Languages of Asia has books on Asian languages, such as Marathi, Bengali, Kannada, Hindi, Sylheti and Sanskrit.
Image credit

Shelf:Wikijunior pre-reader books has Wikijunior books for those at a pre-reader reading level, such as Alphabet, Animal Alphabet, and Food Alphabet, and Small Numbers.
Image credit

Shelf:Computing is a general references shelf for computing, and has books such as Introduction to Computer Information Systems, History of video games, OpenSSH, and A-level Computing.
Image credit

Shelf:Literature has books on literature, the art of written works, such as Muggles' Guide to Harry Potter, Noli Me Tangere, Choosing High Quality Children's Literature, and Writing Adolescent Fiction.
Image credit

Shelf:Constructed languages has books on constructed languages, languages that have been consciously devised, such as Conlang, Mirad Grammar, Esperanto, and Esperanto: A Complete and Comprehensive Grammar.
Image credit

Shelf:Systems engineering has books on systems engineering, such as Control Systems, x86 Disassembly, Circuit Theory, and Electronics.
Image credit

Shelf:Transportation has books on transportation, the movement of people and goods, such as Vehicle Identification Numbers (VIN codes), Automobile Repair, Transportation Deployment Casebook, and Transportation Planning Casebook.
Image credit
